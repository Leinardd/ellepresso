Type & scale

A little moment.

Display: Georgia / serif · 48–64 px

Body: system sans-serif · 16–18 px

Labels: 12–14 px, tracked uppercase

Components

Rounded pill buttons · warm neutral surfaces · clear focus rings

Accessibility: Espresso text on Oat is 12.9:1; Cocoa text on Oat is 5.1:1; Apricot text on Oat is 4.9:1. Body copy is at least 16 px. Interactive controls use visible focus states and generous hit areas.
